Bulk paste
One entry per line: ticker or name first, then the note, e.g. NVDA earnings 11/19, watch data centre revenue or Apple product launch (tab-separated rows copied from a spreadsheet work too). Each note is appended to that row's notes.

Schedule (London time, daylight saving handled automatically)

The UK returns to winter time on 25 October and the US on 1 November. During that week US stocks trade 13:30–20:00 London time; the dashboard adjusts automatically and shows a notice at the top. US market holidays are not skipped automatically; on a holiday you can turn off "Scheduled reasoning" for the day.

Assets and sectors
